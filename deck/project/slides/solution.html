The scope line I would defend: automation only where the outcome is deterministic and reversible by audit trail. Fee component allocation stays human until Finance approves an order (AA-1); it is a switch, not code. The MVP keeps the student-management system as master for students and balances; this tool owns the reconciliation workflow and writes postings to it. Likely question: will this really cut manual work? Answer: P1 and P3 (explicit ID) and P4 (duplicate) need no investigation at all; the queue gives the rest evidence and an owner. I would baseline last month's unmatched count before promising a percentage.
