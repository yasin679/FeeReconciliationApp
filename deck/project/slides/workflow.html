Read left to right: money must be verified before anyone can match or allocate it; allocation is proposed by Operations and approved by a different Finance approver before anything posts. The two teal cells are the control points that stop money being invented: Finance verifying cash or importing the bank feed, and Finance approving someone else's proposal. Admin is deliberately off the money path: they manage roles and policy only, with every change audited. Likely question: what if Finance is busy and approvals pile up? Answer: the queue shows awaiting-approval count and age; thresholds for single approval on small amounts are a Finance policy decision (A-05), not something I would build in by default.
