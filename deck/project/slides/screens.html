I built the wireframes as working screens so the panel can test the logic instead of reading about it. Screen 1 is the operations dashboard: every row shows what is missing and who owns the next step. Screen 2 is what a student sees: only recognised payments reduce the balance; their screenshot claim sits apart as under review, and the P8 reversal shows as an adjustment, not an edit. Screen 3 is where an investigator works: buttons are disabled with the reason whenever a permission, verification or amount check fails.
